Can I play offline?

Core gameplay works offline and needs no account. Adult advertising and privacy forms require a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. This version has no cloud save. Uninstalling Burstloom or clearing its Android storage removes local progress.

How does advertising work?

Adults may choose rewarded rescue and coin bonuses, and see occasional interstitials between completed runs. Under-18 and undisclosed-age profiles are ad-free with equivalent local bonuses. See the privacy policy for details.

How can I delete local data?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Burstloom → Storage → Clear storage. Labels vary by device. This removes local progress and boards; it does not delete data already held by advertising providers.
