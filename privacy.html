About this policy

Gamicia Games develops Burstloom (Android package com.gamiciagames.burstloom). This policy explains the information used by the game, its advertising services, and our support contact. You can play without creating an account. Contact gamiciagames@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Burstloom is intended for players aged 13 and older.

Your game on your device

Burstloom stores your current board and run, scores, coins, purchased powers and boards, tutorial progress, settings, a broad age group, and reward/frequency records in the app's local storage. Offline progress stays on this device and is separate from your optional online wallet. Gamicia Games does not automatically receive your offline save files. The game does not send a separate gameplay analytics feed.

Optional online leaderboard

Online participation starts only when you choose Connect & View Rankings. Supabase creates a guest account with a random user ID and a generated player name; no email, real name, birth date or advertising identifier is requested for that profile. The service receives that ID, difficulty, server-issued run seed, legal move records and verification hashes, scores, online coin awards, and purchases made with earned online coins. Requests also expose ordinary connection information such as IP address to the hosting/authentication service for delivery, security and rate limits.

We use this information to validate runs, maintain separate Beginner, Intermediate and Expert Top 25 tables, display your rank, prevent duplicate awards and maintain your online wallet. Online coins and powers are separate from existing offline balances. Other players see your generated name, avatar, rank and best score, not your session credentials.

Guest access belongs to this installation. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove the credentials needed to regain access; the current game does not offer email-based recovery or account linking. Delete the online account in the game before clearing storage if you want its server data removed. You may continue playing offline without joining online rankings.

Advertising and consent

On first use, Burstloom asks for an age range, with no preselected answer and an option not to answer. Only the broad group is saved on this device; no birth date or exact age is collected. A choice of 13–17 or 18 and older is required to enter gameplay. Under-13 profiles cannot play; unknown or undisclosed profiles remain at the age screen. Players aged 13–17 receive an ad-free experience, including rescue and coin bonuses without watching ads. The game does not initialize the Google advertising or consent services for under-18, unknown or undisclosed profiles. Reset Progress preserves the age choice; clearing the app's storage removes it.

Only adult profiles can initialize Google's AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for interstitial and optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. Adult ad requests request general-audience (G-rated) ad content and disable the publisher first-party identifier. Google applies the applicable consent choices to determine whether personalized, non-personalized or limited ads are eligible; Burstloom does not force personalization or override a refusal. For eligible adult advertising, Burstloom enables the Android Advertising ID permission and delays app measurement until explicit SDK initialization. Google's SDK may collect and share the Android Advertising ID when available, app set ID and other supported device or account identifiers, IP addresses (including approximate location), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Availability and processing depend on Android settings and applicable consent choices. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID through Android's privacy settings. Burstloom does not connect Advertising ID to your online guest profile or verified wallet. The app removes unused Android Ad Services permissions.

Adult ad requests also depend on the privacy status returned by Google's consent service. Where required, a Google privacy form lets you make or change choices. Choosing a rewarded ad does not replace that process. Non-personalized or limited ads still involve data processing. Ordinary gameplay remains available when ads or consent services are unavailable.

Your advertising choices

For adult profiles, Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices reopens Google's form when available for your region. Ad and consent requests are disabled for younger and undisclosed-age profiles. Changing future ad choices does not automatically erase information already held by Google. Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and partner-services explanation (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) describe its practices and controls.

Support messages

If you email gamiciagames@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate a problem and handle your request. Email is processed through our email provider, Google Gmail. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information. The game does not automatically attach your save files or diagnostic logs to an email. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian to contact support and should not send personal details themselves.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local game data remains in app storage until replaced or removed. Reset Progress starts a fresh offline profile but can retain recovery copies; it is not complete data erasure. Android Settings > Apps > Burstloom > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstalling, removes local data and unlocked items. The app disables Android backup for game data.

For an online guest profile, open Home > ONLINE > Delete online profile and confirm deletion. This removes the guest account, online scores, wallet, power/board ownership and stored online run records from our active service, and removes its session and cached profile from this device. Offline progress is preserved. You can also request deletion by emailing gamiciagames@gmail.com with the subject 'Burstloom account deletion' and your generated player name. Do not send access tokens or passwords. We may need reasonable proof of ownership before acting.

Online profile and game records are retained while the account exists unless removed through deletion. Unfinished online runs stop accepting moves after seven days; expiry alone does not delete the account or its records. Supabase may retain operational logs or backups under its service practices and configured retention. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable security or legal obligations; deletion can be requested by email. Google's advertising and consent retention is governed by its policies. Clearing app storage or deleting a Burstloom guest account does not delete data held independently by Google.

Sharing and security

Gamicia Games does not sell your saved progress or support messages. Supabase provides authentication, database and server-function processing for optional online play; the game database is hosted in the United States. Your generated player name, avatar, difficulty, rank and best score can be displayed to other online players. Google processes adult advertising data, and Google Gmail processes support correspondence. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Leaderboard requests use HTTPS. Online session tokens are encrypted in Android's private app storage using a key held by Android Keystore. Local gameplay saves use recovery checks rather than encryption. The server computes scores from legal moves and controls online coin awards and purchases. No storage, transmission or validation method guarantees absolute security. Providers may process information in other countries under their applicable safeguards.

Send requests about information held by Gamicia Games to gamiciagames@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify a request. Use Google's privacy controls for information it holds. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Gamicia Games, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action. Burstloom is not intended for children under 13 and blocks gameplay after an under-13 age choice. Players aged 13–17 can play offline without creating an online profile. Optional online play uses a pseudonymous guest identifier and gameplay data as described here; no email, real name or birth date is required. Parents and guardians can contact us about a child's information or request its deletion.
