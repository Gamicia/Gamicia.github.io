About this policy

Gamicia Games develops Burstloom (Android package com.gamiciagames.burstloom). This policy explains the information used by the game, its advertising services, and our support contact. You can play without creating an account. Contact gamiciagames@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Burstloom is intended for players of all ages.

Your game on your device

Burstloom stores your current board and run, scores, coins, unlocked boards, tutorial progress, settings, a broad age group, and records used to apply ad rewards and control ad frequency in the app's local storage. Local identifiers help prevent duplicate rewards and recover interrupted saves. Gamicia Games does not receive these save files automatically, and this version has no account system or cloud save service. The game does not send a separate gameplay analytics feed to Gamicia Games.

Advertising and consent

On first use, Burstloom asks for an age range, with no preselected answer and an option not to answer. Only the broad group is saved on this device; no birth date or exact age is collected. Players under 18 and players whose age is unknown or undisclosed receive an ad-free experience, including rescue and coin bonuses without watching ads. The game does not initialize the Google advertising or consent services for these profiles. Reset Progress preserves the age choice; clearing the app's storage removes it.

Only adult profiles can initialize Google's AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for interstitial and optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. Adult ad requests request general-audience (G-rated) ad content and disable the publisher first-party identifier. Google applies the applicable consent choices to determine whether personalized, non-personalized or limited ads are eligible; Burstloom does not force personalization or override a refusal. Burstloom removes Android advertising-ID and Ad Services permissions and delays app measurement until explicit SDK initialization. Google's SDK may still process IP addresses (including approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and identifiers such as app set ID where available for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

Adult ad requests also depend on the privacy status returned by Google's consent service. Where required, a Google privacy form lets you make or change choices. Choosing a rewarded ad does not replace that process. Non-personalized or limited ads still involve data processing. Ordinary gameplay remains available when ads or consent services are unavailable.

Your advertising choices

For adult profiles, Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices reopens Google's form when available for your region. Ad and consent requests are disabled for younger and undisclosed-age profiles. Changing future ad choices does not automatically erase information already held by Google. Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and partner-services explanation (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) describe its practices and controls.

Support messages

If you email gamiciagames@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate a problem and handle your request. Email is processed through our email provider, Google Gmail. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information. The game does not automatically attach your save files or diagnostic logs to an email. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian to contact support and should not send personal details themselves.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local game data remains in the app's storage until it is replaced or removed. Reset Progress in Settings starts a fresh game profile but can retain recovery copies and reward records on the device; it is not a complete data-erasure command. To remove the app's stored data, use Android Settings > Apps > Burstloom > Storage > Clear storage (wording may vary), or uninstall the app. This also removes local progress and unlocked boards. Burstloom disables Android app backup for its game data.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable record-keeping, security or legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence. Google's retention and deletion practices for advertising and consent data are governed by its policies and controls. Clearing Burstloom's storage does not delete data Google already holds. There is no Burstloom account to delete.

Sharing and security

Gamicia Games does not sell your saved game progress or support messages. Google and its advertising services process advertising data as described above; service providers process support correspondence to deliver email. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local saves use Android's app storage and recovery checks; these checks are not encryption. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Google may process data in countries other than yours under the safeguards described in its policies.

Requests and younger players

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, withdraw consent, or contact a data-protection authority. Send requests about information held by Gamicia Games to gamiciagames@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify a request. Use Google's privacy controls for information it holds. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Gamicia Games, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action. Burstloom does not require children to submit personal information to Gamicia Games to play. Parents and guardians can contact us about a child's information or request its deletion.
